Long time no see.
I’ve recently been learning acupuncture, and I’m still in the process of studying it.
I have to say, traditional Chinese medicine is far more interesting then I previously thought.
So far, I’ve acupuncture points like 足临泣，太冲，太溪，三阴交，足三里，阳陵泉，阴陵泉，内关，合谷，后溪 on myself。
The downside is that I’m very healthy, so it’s hard to tell if it’s having any effect.
Anyway, it’s still quite fascinating.
